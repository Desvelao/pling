Module pling.notifiers.mqtt

MQTT channel notifier.

Publishes a notification to an MQTT topic for a mqtt destination. Uses luamqtt in its documented one-shot synchronous idiom: open a fresh client, publish on connect, disconnect, then block on mqtt.runsync() until that disconnect completes - no persistent/reused connection, same one-shot-per-send shape as webhooktransport.lua.

Self-contained per destination (broker address, topic, qos, retain, client_id all live on the destination itself).
